Methodology

How the prototype yield layer is generated

This layer is meant for rapid field comparison and demo use. It uses real parcels and real Sentinel-2 reflectance, but the final yield equation is still a prototype transfer and should be treated as yield potential, not calibrated harvest truth.

The calculation procedure is loosely adapted from the Sentinel-2 rice monitoring workflow described in Ali et al. (2021), Integrated method for rice cultivation monitoring using Sentinel-2 data and Leaf Area Index . That paper combines seasonal Sentinel-2 observations with LAI-based yield estimation. Riceify keeps the same empirical logic around NDVI and LAI, but replaces field-survey segmentation with official MAFF parcel geometry and uses a peak-scene proxy for the current municipality-scale prototype.

1. Field geometry

Every parcel on the map comes from the MAFF Fude polygon dataset. The current municipality is official Shibata City (`152064`), and only parcels labeled `rice_field` are included.

2. Peak-scene selection

Riceify searches low-cloud Sentinel-2 Level-2A scenes across the season and chooses a peak-greenness proxy scene using municipality-wide mean NDVI. That follows the paper's general idea of using in-season vegetation behavior rather than a random acquisition date.

3. Band inputs

The prototype yield formula itself uses B04 and B08, while the view switcher exposes additional bands from the same Sentinel-2 scene for visual inspection.

B02: blue band, useful for water and visible-color inspection.
B03: green band, useful for vegetation tone and visible-color inspection.
B04: red band, absorbed by healthy vegetation and used directly in NDVI and SAVI.
B08: near-infrared band, reflected by healthy vegetation and used directly in NDVI and SAVI.

For each field, Riceify computes mean reflectance from the selected scene mosaic inside the parcel footprint before applying the vegetation-index and yield equations.

4. Formula chain

$$NDVI = \frac{B08 - B04}{B08 + B04}$$
$$SAVI = \frac{B08 - B04}{B08 + B04 + 0.5} \times 1.5$$
$$LAI = -\frac{\ln\left(\frac{0.69 - SAVI}{0.59}\right)}{0.91}$$
$$Yield = -3.110 + 1.684 \times LAI + 12.458 \times NDVI$$

In the cited literature, NDVI and LAI are used together as the strongest pre-harvest empirical predictors. Riceify applies that same calculation pattern as a prototype yield-potential layer.

5. Output

The final value shown on the map is labeled prototype yield estimate in `t/ha`. It is useful for ranking fields and spotting spatial patterns, but it still needs Japan-specific calibration before being treated as an operational yield forecast.

Current limitations

This version does not yet use field-level harvest labels, weather, or Sentinel-1 SAR. Some small parcels also fall outside valid satellite coverage in the selected scene, so not every field gets a computed estimate.
